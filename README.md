# SwiftDropCam
Students bridging the gap between work and self management in Cameroon

Problem:
Rapid urbanization and internal migration in Cameroon have led to a growing demographic of young professionals, like Mary, a University of Buea graduate now working in Douala. While these individuals move to seek "greener pastures," they often face a "poverty of time."
Mary relies on her mother for fresh food supplies sent from home, but the current logistics chain is failing her. Between her demanding work schedule and the notorious Douala traffic, Mary cannot reach the bus park in time to collect her parcels. This results in a triple loss: financial waste (transport costs), physical waste (spoiled and moulded foodstuff), and emotional distress (the loss of a connection to home). There is a clear gap in the "last-mile" delivery of perishable
goods between inter-urban bus terminals and the busy professional's doorstep.

Solution:
To resolve this problem of time and money wastage, swiftdrop comes in to bridge the gap between having to work and manage self-care needs. The big question is how?
The platform connects users to delivery agencies, which will collect your delivery items from a specified pickup location to drop them wherever you wish. This will help people like Mary save a lot of time and money, hence helping her work efficiently.
